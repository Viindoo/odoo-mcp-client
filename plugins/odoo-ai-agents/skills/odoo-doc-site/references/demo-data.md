# odoo-doc-site - the data a screenshot shows

A page is written for the readers of its language, so its screens show the business those
readers run. A Vietnamese page shows Vietnamese companies and people, Vietnamese addresses and
amounts in VND; the country is the one the language's locale names, else the one where the
language is mainly spoken. Where the subject itself crosses a border - foreign trade, several
currencies, a group with companies abroad - foreign partners, currencies and languages belong on
the screen.

The capture database is prepared for this once, before any page is captured (the `work: seed`
row), and what was prepared is recorded in `DEMO DATA`, so every writer of the run and every
later run shows the same business. What to prepare and how is the writer's judgement, from what
the planned pages show. Names are fictitious: no real company or person.
